# Day 1 — authority layers

Draft sentences for review against `docs/01-doctrine-baseline.md` (study baseline, 10 November 2026). Keep a sentence only if it still matches that note.

1. **Statute.** The Occupational Safety and Health Act (Public Law 91-596) is a statute, a law passed by Congress. SENTINEL records that layer so a technique publication is never stored as a statute.

2. **Federal regulation.** 29 CFR 1960 is a federal regulation. AR 385-10 says it implements that rule. The current wording is read on ecfr.gov when it is used. This baseline does not restate the regulation.

3. **Army regulation.** AR 385-10 is Department of the Army safety and occupational health policy. Paragraph 1-9 defines ASOHMS and says the mandatory procedures are in DA Pam 385-10.

4. **DA pamphlet.** DA Pam 385-10 is procedures for carrying out a regulation. It does not replace AR 385-10, and it is not a statute.

5. **Doctrine.** ATP 5-19 is risk-management doctrine. It is not a federal regulation, and it is not an Army regulation. Paragraphs 1-45 through 1-48 turn probability and severity into extremely high, high, medium, or low. That result is not a 1-to-5 score.
